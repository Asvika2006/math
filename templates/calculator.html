Calculator Help

  • Addition: Use + (e.g., 2+2)
  • Subtraction: Use - (e.g., 5-3)
  • Multiplication: Use * (e.g., 4*3)
  • Division: Use / (e.g., 10/2)
  • Exponentiation: Use ^ (e.g., 2^3)

  • Trigonometric: sin(x), cos(x), tan(x)
  • Inverse Trig: asin(x), acos(x), atan(x)
  • Logarithmic: log(x) (base 10), ln(x) (natural log)
  • Square Root: sqrt(x)
  • Constants: pi (π), e

Example expressions:

  • sin(pi/2) - Sine of 90 degrees
  • 2*pi*5 - Circumference of circle with radius 5
  • log(1000)/log(10) - Logarithm base 10 of 1000
  • sqrt(16) + 2^3 - Square root of 16 plus 2 cubed
